## All FAQs

### How do I view my exam schedule?

Open the [Student Hub](https://me.northeastern.edu/) and wait for it to load. The exam schedule should show up under the tab bar.

### What is conditional add and drop?

If the “Conditional Add and Drop” checkbox is selected, when you drop a course, the course will only get dropped if another course is added to your schedule.

Use this if you want to replace a course with another course.

### **Are Husky/dining dollars refundable?**

Dining dollars expire at the end of the semester and Husky dollars are refunded when you graduate.

### **How do I know my total awards and financial aid?**

Open the [Financial Aid Status](https://www.pfw.neu.edu/NetPartnerStudent/PgHome.aspx) page. Click the Menu button on the top right, and click "Awards".

The link for awards is available on Husker under Financial.

### **How do I book an appointment with Financial Services?**

Open the [Student Services Portal](https://service.northeastern.edu/appointments) and scroll down to " Student Financial Services". Click on the link most relevant to your case and then follow the steps to book an appointment.

### **If I'm going for co-op in Spring, do my awards and aid get rolled over to the Fall?**

Aid is awarded on a term by term basis. If you are on co-op for spring, you will not be awarded your aid. Your aid will pick back up for Fall if you are enrolled in classes. (Answer from SFS)

### How do I find the deadline to pay fees?

- Open the [E-bill page](https://nu.outsystemsenterprise.com/StudentFinance/ViewBill)
- Click on “View statements”; this will take you to a page on “cashnet.com”
- Click on “Statements” in the sidebar
- Open the latest statement

There is a table that contains the "due date” (deadline) and “current due”.

### Where can I view loans?

[Nelnet](https://secure.nelnet.com/account/login).

### Where can I view all my financial holds?

To see a list of all holds,

- Go to [Registrar Self Service](https://nubanner.neu.edu/ssomanager/c/SSB?pkg=twbkwbis.P_GenMenu%3Fname%3Dbmenu.P_StuMainMnu)
- Click on “Student Account”
- Click on “View Holds”

### Are LEDs allowed?

LED-only string lights.

### What are the features of Husker Courses?

- Ability to search for courses by term, subject, number, and CRN
- View course descriptions and NUPath covered
- View information on course sections including term, professors, timings
- View (and share) course notes

### What are course notes?

Course notes aims to be a place where students can share syllabi, required textbooks, FAQ, and other useful things for a course. They can be found on every course page. An example of a course with good notes is [CS 2500: Fundamentals of Computer Science 1](/CS2500).

If you would like to contribute, look for the “contribute” button, or [fill out this form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdIzBLNUhuc1OMyPCPAKwDBo4gpvqcK78OY9yaoCoJ3YMxTkQ/viewform).
