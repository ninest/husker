### What are the features of Husker Courses?

- Ability to search for courses by term, subject, number, and CRN
- View course descriptions and NUPath covered
- View information on course sections including term, professors, timings
- View (and share) course notes

### What are course notes?

Course notes aims to be a place where students can share syllabi, required textbooks, FAQ, and other useful things for a course. They can be found on every course page. An example of a course with good notes is [CS 2500: Fundamentals of Computer Science 1](/CS2500).
